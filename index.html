About

Adeptrim AI

Adeptrim AI is an enterprise automation, AI optimization, and software consultancy. We design and harden automation programs for regulated organizations — healthcare, clinical research, insurance, and finance — turning brittle, manual workflows into governed, auditable systems that hold up under scrutiny, not just in a demo.

Our focus is the hard part most RPA programs skip: extraction and parsing logic that survives real-world, inconsistently structured source documents, and the governance layer that lets stakeholders trust what the automation is doing. Adeptrim AI is founded and led by Navya Kola, Automation Engineer & RPA Architect.
